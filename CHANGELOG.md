# Changelog — Bedtime Mode Reworked

## 1.1.3
- New: black & white screen during bedtime (Android 15+). Choose separately for scheduled bedtime and for turning it on by hand (Settings → Black & white screen). No extra permissions needed
- Fixed: on unfolded foldables, Settings in the Fancy look couldn't scroll to the bottom of the left column

## 1.1.2
- "Check for updates" (Settings → About) now opens a pop-up: update right there, or see that you're up to date

## 1.1.1
- The "Bedtime is on" notification no longer shows how many notifications are hidden (turn on "Show how many are hidden" in Settings if you want it)
- New option to turn off the "Bedtime is on" notification completely
- Settings are grouped (Look, Schedule, Sound, What to hide, "Bedtime is on" notice, Permissions, About) with simpler wording
- Tap anywhere on a setting to switch it, not just the switch
- Unfolded foldables (and other wide screens) now use two columns even when held upright

## 1.1.0
- New "Fancy" look: a 24-hour dial you drag to set bedtime and wake-up, with everything in big rounded tiles
- Pick one of 8 colours for the Fancy look (Blue, Amber, Coral, Rose, Green, Teal, Lime, Silver)
- Switch between Fancy and Classic anytime ("Fancy look" at the top of Classic, Settings → Look in Fancy)
- History shows the last 3 nights, with "Show all" for the full list
- Tap a night in History to see how many notifications each app had

## 1.0.1
- "Check for updates" now contacts GitHub once per tap and shows which version you have

## 1.0.0
- Hides all notifications at bedtime and brings them back to the shade when it ends
- Keeps the phone silent with its own Do Not Disturb rule (alarms still ring)
- Schedule with start/end time and days
- Quick Settings tile to turn bedtime on/off anytime
- "Bedtime is on" notification with a Turn off button
- History of what was hidden each night
- Allowed apps
- Option to hide or show system notifications
- Own log of everything hidden (7 days), viewable in the app
- Step-by-step setup
- In-app updates
